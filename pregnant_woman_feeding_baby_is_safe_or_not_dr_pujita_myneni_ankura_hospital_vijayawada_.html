Video Monitoring The latest models of baby monitors also feature several other options that takes the pressure off monitoring your baby 24/7. The video monitoring capability of some model allows you to watch over your baby anytime and anywhere in your house. What is a Mobile Baby Monitor? In essence, a mobile baby monitor is a radio transmitter coupled with a receiver. The set is synchronized to a predetermined frequency band that gives off a frequency modulation (FM) signal once it is switched on. More often than not, these portable monitors send out signals between the range of 49,830 MHz and 49,890 MHz. For example, if you want to leave your baby in the living room for a while, you can get the device from the baby s room and transfer it to the living room without going through the difficulty of moving the wires.
